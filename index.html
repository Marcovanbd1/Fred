Meet your teacher

Fred Burnham

Fred is an Advanced Nurse Practitioner and Prescribing Lead here in Weymouth & Portland, with almost 30 years in the NHS.

He has a particular interest in persistent pain, musculoskeletal health and lifestyle medicine, and has helped develop non-medication approaches to chronic pain in the NHS.

Fred has practised yoga for nearly ten years in the Viniyoga tradition, which adapts the practice to the person rather than expecting the person to fit the practice. Whatever your age, fitness or ability, he'll help you find a way in.

  • Advanced Nurse Practitioner
  • Viniyoga Pranayama Teacher
  • Yoga therapy training
Is it for me?

Gentle, welcoming and adapted to you

This isn't a fitness class. Sessions mix gentle movement, simple breathing and relaxation, with choices for every body.

It may help if you'd like to

  • Move more comfortably and with confidence
  • Stay active while living with persistent pain
  • Feel calmer and manage everyday stress
  • Sleep better and have more energy
  • Learn simple practices to use at home

Before you book

  • You'll need to get down to the floor and back up on your own
  • Tell Fred about any health condition, injury, operation, pregnancy, dizziness or balance problem
  • Please plan to come to all six sessions
  • Yoga isn't a cure and doesn't replace medical care

In feedback from the last group, the clearest improvements were in sleep, energy, feeling calmer, focus and confidence. This was a small, early evaluation, so it can't prove yoga caused the changes.

NICE recommends supervised group exercise for people living with persistent pain, and research suggests yoga can help some people with back pain, stress and sleep, though results vary. Read more from NICE and Cochrane.
